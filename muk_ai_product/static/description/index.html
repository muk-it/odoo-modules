From the list to the shop

Three skills for your products

The Product Content Writer agent comes with the module. Start a chat, pick one of its suggestions or type / for a skill. The General Assistant can use the skills too.

Add products

From a price list or a link: the vendor is found or created, every item matched by the vendor's code, barcode or reference, new products created and changed prices updated.

Long lists in the sandbox

A list of more than a few rows is read and cleaned in the code sandbox of OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. Approve the first product with Allow for session and the rest follows.

Complete the product

Looks the product up by its manufacturer code, barcode or name, reads the manufacturer's page or datasheet, and writes the name, the descriptions and the shop texts in every product language.

Translate or rewrite

Ask in the chat: a missing language in the trade's own terms, or a description shorter or more technical. Facts stay, none is added.

Image variants

A packshot on white, the product in use and a detail, made from the main photo and added as extra images marked as AI-generated.

Never generated

Barcodes, references, manufacturer data, energy labels and safety information are only copied from a source that states them. Empty ones are named as gaps.

Price lists and links

From the supplier's list to the shop page

Attach the price list to a chat with the writer and pick Add products. A long list is read in the code sandbox of your AI provider: the header row is found, headings, totals and footer text are skipped, and every row is cleaned. Each item is matched against your products by the vendor's code, the barcode or the internal reference, and you see three groups: new products, products whose vendor price changes, and unchanged ones.

After your go, new products are created with their vendor, code, price, minimum quantity and lead time, and changed prices are updated, never duplicated. A shop link works the same way, and the product photo comes along. Then Complete the product writes the texts in every language from the facts of the page.

Images

More pictures of the product, from the photo you have

With the main photo as reference, the image model shows the same product in new scenes: a packshot on white, the product in use, a close-up of a defining detail. No logo, no text and no face is drawn.

You pick the ones to keep. They are added as extra images, marked as AI-generated, and the main photo stays as it is unless you ask for one to replace it.

Requirements

Odoo.sh or on-premise, Community or Enterprise, with MuK AI Assistant and a chat model, and an image model for the image variants. Long price lists need a provider whose code sandbox reads the chat's files: OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. eCommerce is optional and adds its fields.

Configuration

Nothing to set up beyond MuK AI: the texts are written in every active language, and the agent asks before every change.

Installation

Depends on muk_ai_skills and muk_product. Install it like any other module; the Product Content Writer and its three skills come with it.

Usage

Start a chat, attach a price list or paste a link and pick Add products; on a product, pick Complete this product. Every new product, price and language waits for your approval.

Upgrade

An upgrade keeps your settings and the changes you made to the agent and its skills.
