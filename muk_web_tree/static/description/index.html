Nest your records under their parents, in one list.

A new view type for any model that points to itself — categories, contacts, departments, locations. Rows open like folders, and everything a list can do, from inline editing to sums, still works.

What it adds

A list that knows its hierarchy

Everything else is the list view you already know: columns, widgets, sums, buttons, decorations and inline editing.

Rows that nest

Each record sits indented under its parent, with a chevron to open it. The rows you opened stay open the next time.

Search in place

Matches stay in the tree. The parents that lead to them are shown greyed out and opened for you.

Add a child on the row

The + on a row starts a record right under it, with the parent filled in, edited inline or in the form.

Move by drag and drop

Drop a row on another to move it beneath, or on its upper or lower edge to make the two siblings.

Totals of a branch

Mark a numeric column as a rollup and every parent row shows the total of its whole subtree.

Keys and screen readers

Right opens a row, Left closes it or jumps to its parent, and each row tells assistive technology its level.

Searching

Find a record without losing its place

A flat search result tells you what matched and nothing about where it lives. The tree list keeps every match at its own depth and brings the parents along, greyed out and in italics, opened down to the match. Clear the search and the tree is back as you left it.

Editing

Add it where it belongs

Hover a row and click + to start a record directly beneath it, with the parent already set. On an editable tree you type into the row and save; otherwise the form opens, prefilled. Moving a record later is a drag of its handle onto the new parent.

No loops

A record can not be dropped onto one of its own descendants, so a drag never cuts a branch off the tree.

Where it stops

Put it on any hierarchy

The module brings the view type, not the views. A <treelist> goes next to the list and form an action already has, on whichever models your teams browse as a hierarchy.

Any parent field

The tree follows parent_id, or any many2one to the same model named in parent_field.

Rollups on your numbers

Add rollup="1" to a numeric field and parents show the total of their subtree, in the row and in the footer.

A tree in every group

Group the view by a field and every group shows its records as a tree, with its own pager for the top-level rows.
