What it adds

Two entries, where you already look

No new button, no keyboard trick to remember. They sit in the cog menu of the control panel, and only while the view is grouped.

Expand All

Unfolds every group of the view, then every group inside those, until the records are on screen.

Collapse All

Folds the tree back from the innermost level up, leaving the view as it was when you grouped it.

As deep as the grouping

Two fields, three fields, more: each level is opened in turn until there is nothing folded left.

Only where they apply

They appear the moment a list or kanban is grouped and go again when the grouping is removed.

Contacts  ›  Country  ›  Salesperson
Grouped by country and then by salesperson, after one click on Expand All.
Nested groups

All the way down

Odoo can open the groups you can see. A view grouped by two fields then leaves you with a second level still folded, and a third after that. Expand All keeps going until every group is open and the records are in front of you.

One request per level

A whole level is marked open before anything is fetched, so twenty groups cost one read, not twenty.
