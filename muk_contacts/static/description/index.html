What it adds

An address book that stays in order

The contacts app you already use, with the fields and views a large address book needs.

Contact numbers

Drawn from a sequence when a company or person is created, unique, and handed down to its people and addresses.

Find by number

Type a contact number wherever a contact is searched: the search bar, a contact field or the merge wizard.

Default addresses

Choose which address receives invoices and which one deliveries, instead of relying on the first one found.

See what you pick

Contact suggestions show whether each entry is a company, a person, an invoice or a delivery address.

More in every row

Address type, notes, portal or internal user and the company, each as an optional column of the list.

Contacts as a tree

Contacts open as a tree, ahead of kanban and list: companies open to their people and addresses, and a drag moves them.

The list

Read a contact without opening it

The contact list shows the number, the company and the name in their own columns. An icon tells companies, persons and invoice and delivery addresses apart, addresses are also coloured, companies are set in bold, and a click on the notes icon shows the internal notes in place. The search panel filters by country and tag, and new filters pick out main contacts, portal users and internal users.

The form

One number, one address to send to

The contact number sits above the address. A contact created while the sequence was switched off gets one with the button beside the field. On the Sales and Purchase tab, the default invoice and delivery address are picked from the contact's own addresses.

Kept through a merge

Merge a numbered duplicate into a contact without a number, and the contact that stays takes the number over.
