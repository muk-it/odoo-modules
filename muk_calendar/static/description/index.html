What it does

Dates you already have, where people look

Odoo shows time off in the calendar and stops there. This module does the same for any record with a date, and lets every calendar leave Odoo as a subscription.

Records as events

A model, a date, an optional end date or duration, and a filter decide what the calendar shows.

Follows every change

Each change to a record updates its event, and a nightly run catches everything else.

Attendees from the record

A contact or user field becomes the attendees, so assignees see their deadlines in their own calendar.

Read as the owner

The calendar owner's rights pick the records, and every viewer only sees the events of records they may open.

One click to the record

An event cannot drift from its record: it is read-only in the calendar and opens the record it shows.

iCal for every calendar

Personal or record calendar, each gets a secret link with all details or busy times only.

Setup

A calendar from four fields

An administrator creates a calendar from the side panel of the calendar, or opens an existing one, chooses the model and the date its records are placed on, and narrows them with the filter editor Odoo uses everywhere. All day turns a date and time into a day, the attendees field brings the people along, and the calendar is shared with the users who should see it.

In the calendar

The record stays the source

The events are real calendar events, so colours, filters, sharing and synchronisation work as for any meeting. They cannot be moved in the calendar: the date belongs to the record, and one click opens it there. Deadlines are shown as free time, so they never block a booking.

iCal sharing

One link, any calendar app

The owner of a calendar opens it from the side panel, creates the link and copies it into Google, Apple or Outlook as a subscribed calendar. A new link replaces the old one, and sharing stops with one click.

Busy times only

For a link that leaves the team, subscribers see when you are busy and nothing else — no titles, no descriptions, no places.
