What it adds

The product app Odoo leaves out

Odoo keeps products inside Sales, Purchase and Inventory. This module gives them an app of their own, one click from the home menu.

Every product menu

Products, variants, pricelists, documents, combo choices, units, packaging barcodes and base units.

References and barcodes

Sequences fill in missing codes, barcodes carry an EAN-13 check digit and references stay unique.

Bulk search

Paste values split by line, space, comma, semicolon or tab and match them exactly or in part.

Manufacturer details

Manufacturer, product name, code and URL, and the code is found by every product search.

Search panels

Filter products and variants by company, category, type, attribute and tag in one click.

Fixed prices in one list

The fixed prices of every pricelist and product, edited inline like a spreadsheet.

Bulk search

Paste a list, open those products

Copy a column from a spreadsheet, an order or an email and paste it. Search by internal reference, name or barcode, exactly or in part. The preview shows the first matches before the list opens.

Manufacturer

Who makes it, under which code

Each product records its manufacturer and the manufacturer’s own name, code and URL. The code belongs to the variant, and typing it into any product search finds the product.

Settings

Switch features on from the app

The app has its own settings tab: variants, pricelists, units and packagings, base unit prices and the two automations. A link under each option opens its configuration.

No Sales app required

Odoo shows these options only inside Sales, Purchase, Inventory or eCommerce. Here they come with the product app alone.
